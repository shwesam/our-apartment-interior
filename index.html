LATEST MASTER LAYOUT

01 — MASTER PLAN

The apartment, mapped first.

The latest visual includes the updated Bedroom 1 dressing label, Bedroom 3 blue bedding, the foyer shoe-rack concept, integrated bedroom couch storage and the compact kitchen planning.

02 — LIVING AREA

Elegant, modern, with a little heritage.

The living space stays uncluttered, centered around the sectional sofa and the custom TV wall, using white, marble, champagne and selective wood accents in the latest combined concept.

03 — KITCHEN

11' × 7' — storage first, style second.

The main run stays true to the layout: refrigerator → storage cabinets → sink → cylinder/RO → open shelf, with the stove/chimney on the right wall; the separate wall behind the bathroom becomes full-height storage to the ceiling.

04 — FOYER

A small Indian-inspired welcome zone.

The latest foyer concept fits the compact 5'4½" × 4'9" zone: a small entrance shoe rack with attached stool, round illuminated mirror, botanical/kolam-inspired backdrop and warm layered lighting.

05 — DINING + CROCKERY

Dining only when needed.

The crockery unit sits beside the refrigerator with a pull-down dining surface, a mix of closed and display storage, and lower utility storage for cookware and kitchen supplies.

06 — PUJA

A compact sacred unit with a pocket door.

The puja niche uses multiple shelves for idols, a pull-out prasad tray, dedicated drawers and a sliding door that disappears into the wall pocket.

07 — BEDROOMS

Three bedrooms, three personalities.

Every bedroom keeps the actual footprint and orientation; wardrobes use the builder-provided zones where applicable, and furniture is kept proportionate to the rooms.

BEDROOM 1 • 11' × 10'

Pastel green + champagne

Wardrobe remains only in the ALMIRAH zone; the remaining wall is a clean dresser/vanity with a large illuminated mirror and no open glass storage.

BEDROOM 2 • 10'9" × 8'

Light dusty blue + warm beige

The existing brick wardrobe is retained and simply closed with custom shutters, keeping the small room light and uncluttered.

BEDROOM 3 • 10'9" × 12'7½"

Blue + warm taupe with built-in couch

The bed sits with the window at the head side, the wardrobe remains in the designated zone, and the remaining run becomes an integrated couch with storage.

08 — TOILETS

Compact wet/dry planning.

The bathroom boards show shower zoning, toilet placement and compact vanity ideas sized around the actual footprints.

09 — BALCONY

Utility balcony, kept open and practical.

The washing machine stays at the right end, with overhead storage, mop/broom organization, pull-out cleaning storage and a foldable drying rack while keeping the open grill side and living-room French window clear.

DESIGN SYSTEM

One apartment.
One visual language.

Warm neutrals, selective pastels, black accents, practical storage, soft lighting and a few Indian heritage details tie the home together without making every room identical.
